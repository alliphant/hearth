# Marketplace seller fees — what each platform actually takes

Reference for figuring **net proceeds** (what the seller clears after the
marketplace's cut), not just the sticker price. Verified June 2026 against
each platform's official fee page. Fees change — re-check the official pages
(linked at the bottom) before quoting them as gospel.

## The cut, by platform

| Platform | Seller fee | Applies to | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **eBay** | ~**13.6%** final value (most categories); **15.3%** clothing/shoes/accessories (2026) + **$0.30** per order (≤$10) or **$0.40** (>$10) | item price **+ shipping + tax** | Store subscriptions lower the % (≈2.5–14.95% by category). Promoted Listings ad rate is extra and optional. |
| **Poshmark** | **$2.95 flat** under $15; **20%** at $15 and up | **item price only** | No listing, monthly, or separate processing fee. Free prepaid USPS Priority label (buyer pays the $7.67 shipping). |
| **Facebook Marketplace** | **0%** local pickup; **5%** (min $0.40) on shipped orders via Checkout | shipped: item + shipping | **Linda's FB listings are local pickup only → $0 fee.** That's FB's structural edge for local items. |
| **Mercari** | **10% flat** | item price **+ buyer-paid shipping** | No seller processing fee, no ACH withdrawal fee (since Jan 2025). Buyer pays a separate 3.6% protection fee. |
| **Depop (US)** | **0% selling** + **3.3% + $0.45** payment processing | item + shipping | US selling fee removed in 2024. Lowest total cut of the fashion platforms. |

## Net-proceeds math

`net = sale_price − platform_fee − cost_basis − your_shipping_cost`

Worked examples on a **$50** item (item price only, ignoring shipping/tax):

- **Facebook (local):** $50 − $0 = **$50** clear.
- **Depop:** $50 − ($1.65 + $0.45) = **$47.90**.
- **eBay (non-apparel):** $50 − ($6.80 + $0.40) = **$42.80**.
- **eBay (apparel 15.3%):** $50 − ($7.65 + $0.40) = **$41.95**.
- **Mercari:** $50 − $5.00 = **$45.00**.
- **Poshmark:** $50 − $10.00 = **$40.00**.

So on a straight-fee basis the same $50 sale clears anywhere from **$40
(Poshmark)** to **$50 (FB local)**. That spread is real money over a year of
sales — it's why platform choice is a pricing decision, not just a reach
decision.

## How to use this when advising a price

- **Quote the seller her NET, not the list price.** "List at $52 on Poshmark
  and you'll clear about $42 after their 20%" lands better than a number she
  has to discount in her head later.
- **Lean local/FB for bulky or low-margin items** — no fee and no shipping
  hassle. Lean Poshmark/eBay for searchable, shippable, higher-value items
  where the reach is worth the cut.
- **Poshmark's $2.95 flat under $15** makes it the best home for cheap items
  (a $12 sale keeps $9.05 — 75%); above $15 the 20% bites and eBay/Mercari
  often net more.
- **eBay's apparel rate is now 15.3%** (raised from 15% in 2026) — for
  clothing it's no longer clearly cheaper than Poshmark; decide on reach and
  the item's searchability, not fees alone.

The system auto-estimates these fees on the seller's office (profit/margin
use them when she hasn't entered an exact number); she can always record the
real fee to override.
